Bulk Call Analysis: UI mockups

Layout B, where the screen follows the job: chat is the main pane while you set a job up, and a large job view takes over once the full run starts. All names and numbers are made up, and quotes are shown masked exactly as the reader sees them.

Plan in chat

You describe what you want in plain words. Claude checks the data (read-only, masked), then puts a plan card in the chat. Nothing is pulled or read until you press Approve plan. The right-hand rail shows where the job is and the files it has made so far.

Full run: the job view takes over

Once you press Start, the main pane becomes the job view and chat moves into a drawer on the right, which you can close to a thin tab. Everything here comes from real activity: the time left is worked out from the actual reading speed over the last 30 minutes, leaving out pauses and sleep, and "last activity" turns into a warning if nothing happens for 3 minutes.

When something happens

Each of these replaces the "Reading" line at the top of the job view, and the important ones also pop up as a Windows notification. The rule is always the same: say what happened, what the app is doing about it, and whether anything was lost.

Paused: ChatGPT allowance at 95%Paused

The app stopped sending new leads so it never runs onto paid credits. It starts again on its own when the allowance resets: Tue 24 Nov, 09:00 (in 3 days 15 h). 6,400 leads are waiting, and nothing was lost.

Open folder
The laptop was asleep (17:12 – 17:40)Reading

Sleep doesn't count as a stall. The 6 leads being read at the time went back in the queue, and nothing was lost. The time left leaves out the 28 minutes.

On battery: 64%, about 2 h leftReading

Still reading. Below 20% the app stops starting new leads and pauses cleanly. Plug in to keep going overnight; it carries on by itself when power returns.

Stalled: no reply for 3 minutesStalled

6 leads are in progress, but the reader hasn't answered. The internet connection may be down. Leads don't count as failed while the connection is down.

PauseShow details
Paused to avoid paid creditsPaused

ChatGPT reported a credit charge. Every lead in progress was stopped at once and put back in the queue (no tries used up). Check that automatic top-up is off in ChatGPT, then resume.
